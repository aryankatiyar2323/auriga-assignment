# AI Logs

## Summary of AI-Assisted Development

This project was developed with AI assistance during the Auriga IT Round 2 Build Round.

### Problem Understanding

The AI and candidate analyzed the given problem of managing contributions toward a shared farewell gift. The core requirements identified were:

- Configurable pool budget
- Multiple participants
- Equal contribution calculation
- Full and partial payments
- Overpayments
- Tracking total collection
- Tracking remaining amount
- Individual balances
- Simplified settlement instructions showing who should pay whom

The project was designed as a reusable group settlement application rather than being limited to the farewell-gift example.

### Project Direction

The project was named **GiftPool — Group Gift Settlement Manager**.

The application was planned around the following workflow:

```text
Configure Pool
      ↓
Add Participants
      ↓
Calculate Equal Share
      ↓
Record Payments
      ↓
Calculate Balances
      ↓
Track Collection Progress
      ↓
Generate Settlements
